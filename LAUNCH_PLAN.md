## Official competition timeline

The full competition runs from the upcoming launch through **Show Week in May
2027**, not merely through the initial promoter campaign.

1. **November–December 2026 — Launch & Promotion:** website launch, public
   awareness, promoter recruitment and Christmas promotion.
2. **Stage 1 — Create & Submit:** all-sports signature move cards, video
   submissions and collections; recreate professional moves or submit originals.
3. **Stage 2 — Signature Move Showdown:** community voting and qualifying matchups.
4. **Stage 3 — Championship Brackets:** qualified participants compete toward
   the championship.
5. **May 2027 — Show Week:** Championship Final Faceoff, verified official
   winner announcement, Physical Champion Card award and celebration.

Exact Show Week dates and individual stage deadlines await owner confirmation.
Do not automatically close the competition on December 16. The initial target
is 500 AI-generated, owner-approved professional athlete cards; that target is
not a claim that 500 cards are already generated, verified or published.

Entry and community voting are free. The optional motion card costs $2 plus
applicable tax. Purchases never change contest eligibility or results.

This repository-local plan consolidates the revised campaign window and the
previously reported promoter outreach. The dates and targets are planning
assumptions, not authorization to launch or send email.

## Launch goals

- Recruit, approve, and train a target of 5,000 promoters.
- Complete the promoter website, registration, assignments, dashboards,
  referral links, payment verification, and commission tracking.
- Keep the commercial promoter campaign separate from competition activation;
  Stage 1 and competition features may be activated later with owner approval.
- Obtain final owner approval before launch.

## Proposed promoter campaign

- **Campaign window:** December 7–16, 2026, inclusive.
- **Recruitment target:** 5,000 promoters, all confirmed and trained before launch.
- **Commission:** $0.96 per verified, attributed $2 motion-card purchase.
- **Per-promoter limits:** $96 per day and $960 over the 10-day campaign.
- Payments are processed online; promoters do not collect cash.
- There is no mandatory 100-card daily quota.
- Merchandise commissions remain unconfirmed and must not be automated until the
  formula is approved separately.

### No-duplicate outreach check

Before sending any further recruitment invitation:

1. Reconcile the recipient-level records from both reported batches and the
   522-contact review.
2. Deduplicate by recipient and organization; preserve the prior contacted,
   backup, held, and excluded classifications.
3. Check available sending and bounce records. Gmail acceptance alone does not
   confirm delivery.
4. Report distinct contacts, confirmed sends, delivery outcomes, skips, and
   unknown statuses separately. Do not count an email recipient as a promoter
   unless their application, approval, and training are recorded.
5. Do not resend to previously contacted, held, or excluded recipients until an
   owner reviews the reconciled records.
