The project scope includes a large, expandable professional athlete move-card library. Stage 1 is professional athlete move-recreation challenges: participants can search by athlete, sport, or signature move, choose “Cover This Move,” and submit attempts linked to the selected professional card. Multiple participants may attempt the same move.

Cards generated with AI from reference videos require accurate signature-move identification and owner verification, editing, and publishing controls. Preserve the existing card design and competition system.

**Why:** The user explicitly approved this Stage 1 feature and added its requirements to the Cup of Tea project scope.

**How to apply:** Treat reference-based AI suggestions as drafts until an owner verifies and publishes them. Keep many submissions attached to one professional card; do not merge participant attempts into official standings or results unless existing competition rules explicitly do so.
