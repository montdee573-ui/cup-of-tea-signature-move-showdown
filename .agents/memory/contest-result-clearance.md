---
name: Contest result clearance
description: The user's required handling for contest data and official results.
---

Use both the original Cup of Tea app and Viral Reel in the contest workflow. An actual final result requires owner clearance before it is declared or sent by email. Do not present a concept or simulated outcome as an official result; base an official result on verified contest data.

**Why:** the user asked to use both apps and said actual finals should be cleared before sharing while the experience is new.

**How to apply:** preserve source and verification status when handling contest data. If the apps are not connected or vote data are unavailable, report that clearly instead of inventing a winner. Email a final only after approval and once the destination and delivery path are configured.
