# The Champion’s Brew Loop — alternate campaign film

## Director's treatment
**Promise:** The community makes the matchup; everyone can enter and vote for free.  
**Identity:** Midnight ink, electric cyan and violet drawn from the supplied Cup of Tea campaign key art, with a small gilded crown accent. Space Grotesk display and DM Sans support copy.  
**Hero:** The campaign's illustrated champion cup and two signature-move cards, treated as a kinetic loop mechanism rather than a conventional announcement.  
**Motion:** Card-swipe split reveals; cup-surface orbit/aperture; typographic impact cuts with a light-trace carrier.  
**Arc:** A matchup appears from the dark, becomes a community vote, then resolves into the cup emblem and truthful free-entry promise. The finale's icon-sized cup is a different scale and role from the opening's oversized artwork, and its orbit becomes the opener's card edge.  
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, for YouTube Shorts; protected copy stays in the center 80% height.  
**Payoff:** A match becomes a shared ritual, not an invented official result. No champion, date, purchase, or result is claimed.

## Shot-by-shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.000–4.444s / 4.444s — “TWO MOVES.”
**Purpose:** Hook on the feeling of a head-to-head community exhibition, without suggesting official bracket standings.
**Exact content:** 0.35s: “TWO MOVES.” 2.05s: small “A COMMUNITY SHOWDOWN” holds to 3.75s.
**Composition:** Midnight field. Open on a cropped electric cyan border at left and magenta border at right, each card slanted toward a narrow center seam; no human likeness is introduced beyond existing art. Main phrase occupies central safe region in cream, about 15vmin; cyan and pink own separate halves.
**Artistic idea:** Two card-frame corners behave like opposing blades; their near-collision creates the exact glowing seam that becomes the cup's orbit.
**Choreography:** 0.00–0.25 cyan edge is already visible and snaps 7% inward; 0.45s magenta edge answers with a stiff snap; 0.75s two narrow rays converge. 0.95s “TWO” punches in, 1.35s “MOVES.” locks under it. At 2.1s the split compresses toward center while the cards rotate a few degrees toward level; at 3.25s seam bends into a circular arc and enlarges to carry the cut.
**Transition contract:** The seam's cyan-to-magenta arc overlays the next shot's illustrated cup rim; a short scale-through and split-mask overlap make the transition compound.
**Assets/technique:** CSS geometry and campaign key art; large masked text; multi-panel split, clip path, scale carrier.
**Sound:** Fallback locally synthesized instrumental; 108 BPM, cut near 4.444s (beat 9); callback music generation could not run because paid-mode confirmation was unavailable.

### Shot 02 / 4.444–8.889s / 4.445s — “THE COMMUNITY DECIDES.”
**Purpose:** Show the product/campaign mechanism: matchups as community exhibitions.
**Exact content:** 4.55s: “THE COMMUNITY” then 5.10s “DECIDES.”; at 7.20s micro-label “COMMUNITY EXHIBITION” holds to 8.85s.
**Composition:** Campaign artwork fills the vertical frame with cup centered low and card silhouettes at upper sides. Its colorful steam is the focal axis; headline sits in a dark narrow top band, no overlap with the cup emblem.
**Artistic idea:** The incoming seam becomes a moving orbit around the printed cup; its geometry unlocks a portrait crop of the square art, creating a designed portal rather than simply zooming a still.
**Choreography:** 0.0–0.6s arc opens to a full circular aperture; image scales 1.12 to 1.0 and settles from -2deg. 0.7s headline unmasks in two lines. 1.8s cyan card fragment tips toward the center; 2.35s magenta fragment counters it. 3.0s orbit ring traces a half-turn, 4.0s a spark follows the track and collapses to the center for the vote ripple.
**Transition contract:** Spark becomes the center of the next shot's expanding vote ripple; type shears away as the image plane scales through and fades.
**Assets/technique:** Existing campaign image, portrait crop via object-fit cover; ring SVG/CSS; split reveal and perspective card fragments. No generated footage.
**Sound:** Instrumental lift; cut at 8.889s (beat 17); locally synthesized beat grid, not an analyzed generated track.

### Shot 03 / 8.889–14.444s / 5.555s — “ENTRY IS FREE. VOTING IS FREE.”
**Purpose:** Put the confirmed core promise first and unmistakably on screen.
**Exact content:** 9.25s: “ENTRY IS” 9.75s: “FREE.” 11.05s: “VOTING IS” 11.55s: “FREE.” Small 12.9s footer: “Optional creator reel is separate.” through 13.8s.
**Composition:** Flat ink-blue field with two cropped card corners in opposite corners. Oversized cream typography stacked center with cyan and magenta vote marks alternating left/right. Footer sits in bottom of safe area, visually subordinate.
**Artistic idea:** Two voting marks arrive from opposing cards and stamp the words “FREE.”; a split-color underline snaps into one shared gold spark, framing the message instead of decorating it.
**Choreography:** 0.0s spark opens a ripple, 0.25s old image vanishes behind a two-panel split. 0.4s “ENTRY IS” reveals, 0.9s cyan mark stamps beside “FREE.” at 1.05s. 2.0s new line replaces it via vertical mask; 2.65s magenta mark stamps beside second “FREE.” At 3.85s the separate-reel footer fades in; at 4.4s marks lift and converge into one gold spark, moving upward into the final lockup.
**Transition contract:** The gold spark stays on the same coordinate, scales into the crown accent in the campaign art, and incoming art aperture opens around it while old type splits away.
**Assets/technique:** SVG vote-stamp graphics and masks, palette from key art. None implies a ballot tally or result.
**Sound:** Beat accents land at 8.889, 11.111, and 13.333s (beats 17, 21, 25).

### Shot 04 / 14.444–20.000s / 5.556s — “SHOW UP. VOTE.”
