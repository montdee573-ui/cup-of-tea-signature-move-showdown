## Status and contest separation

The physical champion-card prize is paused and inactive. The current public Cup of Tea rules describe a free community-recognition challenge with era-bracket champions; they do not establish a cross-era final or a physical prize. These proposed prize rules do not change those rules.

The original Cup of Tea card-battle app on Base44 is the intended official source for brackets and official results. Viral Reel's Replit pairwise showdowns are separate community exhibitions; they do not select the official champion or determine prize eligibility. The owner must manually verify any official result in Base44 before notifying a possible winner.

**Public rules source checked October 31, 2026:** [tea-clash-cards.base44.app/rules](https://tea-clash-cards.base44.app/rules). Confirm that this source and its wording are still current before any publication.

## 4. Entry period and how to enter

The proposed entry period begins **[opening date, time, and time zone]** and ends **[closing date, time, and time zone]** (“Entry Period”). Entries received outside this period are not eligible unless the final published rules expressly say otherwise.

## 5. Voting windows, vote limits, and advancement

The current Base44 rules provide a 24-hour voting window for each matchup. For the proposed cross-era final, the proposed voting window is also **24 hours from the time that final opens**.

For every matchup, the official page must show the opening and closing date, time, and time zone. Voting closes at the displayed closing time; votes submitted after that time do not count. If the platform is unavailable or a vote cannot be recorded, the Sponsor must follow a counsel-reviewed outage procedure stated before launch.

The current Base44 rules allow one community like per device per matchup. The Sponsor must confirm that this remains the intended vote limit, how invalid or automated votes will be handled, and what reliable record will establish the closing totals.

The existing competition has three era brackets: **80s Legends, 90s Apex, and 00s Innovation**. Its currently published format advances the winner through head-to-head rounds and recognizes a champion in each era bracket. The proposed physical-prize winner would be selected in a separate cross-era final among the three verified era champions. Only the Base44 bracket and final, as updated and approved before launch, may determine that proposed winner.

**Exact Entry Period dates and final opening/closing timestamps are not yet set. They are launch blockers, not details to fill in after promotion.**

## 6. Ties and result records

Under the currently published Base44 rules, a vote tie in an era-bracket matchup is broken by the higher C.O.R.E. execution-fidelity score. The final rules must define how that score is recorded and applied consistently.

For the proposed cross-era final, the proposed tie process is:

1. If the final vote totals are tied, the finalist with the higher recorded C.O.R.E. execution-fidelity score wins.
2. If the C.O.R.E. scores are also tied or unavailable, the Sponsor will open a 24-hour tie-break vote between the tied finalists and apply the same score rule if that vote is tied.
3. If the tie-break vote and scores are still tied or cannot be verified, no winner will be declared until the Sponsor follows a further tie procedure that has been approved by counsel and published before the final opens.

The Sponsor must retain the official matchup, closing vote totals, applicable C.O.R.E. scores, and verification notes. The Sponsor must not resolve a tie through an unpublished or ad hoc choice.

**Review item:** Confirm that the proposed tie-break sequence is workable in Base44, publish the final tie method before any final voting begins, and state what happens if the platform cannot produce a reliable score or vote record.
