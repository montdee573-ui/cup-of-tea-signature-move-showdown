# Cup of Tea Signature Move Showdown — Champion Card Prize Draft

> **DRAFT FOR OWNER AND QUALIFIED COUNSEL REVIEW — NOT ACTIVE OFFICIAL RULES.**
> This draft is informational, is not legal advice, and must not be used to announce or promote a prize until the organizer and qualified counsel approve the complete official rules.

## 1. Free entry and voting

Entry and voting in the Signature Move Showdown are free. No purchase, paid motion card, merchandise order, or other payment is required to enter, vote, remain eligible, or improve a participant's chance of winning. Replit's current pairwise matchups are community exhibitions only and do not decide the official champion or physical-prize eligibility.

## 2. Official contest and result source

The original Cup of Tea card-battle app on Base44 is the intended system of record for official brackets and results. The contest owner must manually verify the final result in Base44 before identifying a prize winner. No result from Replit's pairwise matchups, paid-card flow, or merchandise activity may replace or alter that result.

The currently published Base44 rules describe three era brackets—80s Legends, 90s Apex, and 00s Innovation—using head-to-head matchups, 24-hour voting periods, community likes, and a C.O.R.E. execution-fidelity score to break ties. Those rules currently award era-bracket recognition only; they do not establish a cross-era final or a physical-card prize.

## 3. Proposed overall champion selection

The proposed prize winner is one overall champion across the competition categories. The three verified era champions would compete in a new cross-era final in Base44. The owner must verify that final and record the official result before notifying anyone or beginning fulfillment.

**Owner and counsel must confirm before publication:** the final's voting window, tie procedure, eligibility cut-off, result-record format, and any permitted disqualification or replacement procedure.

## 4. Proposed prize

One eligible overall champion would receive one printed physical card based on their final, owner-approved Base44 champion-card artwork. The prize is awarded without a purchase requirement and has no cash alternative unless the final approved rules expressly provide otherwise.

The organizer has paused physical prize setup. The intended format remains one single printed card, but Printful's catalog exposes a poker playing-card product (2.5″ × 3.5″), not a single-card order. Do not switch printers, substitute a deck, or prepare a production mockup unless the organizer re-approves the physical prize format.

The prize design, print specification, proof, and winner must be approved before any order is placed. No mockup using celebrity reference art is approved for production; reference cards may guide an original frame only.

## 5. Proposed notification and delivery responsibility

The contest owner is responsible for checking the Base44 result, contacting the verified winner, obtaining the winner's acceptance, and arranging delivery of the single card at no cost to the winner.

Before publication, the organizer must choose and state:

- the official contact method used to notify the winner;
- how long the winner has to respond and what happens if they do not;
- the eligible shipping destinations and any age or residency requirements;
- the delivery address collection method, shipping coverage, and expected timing;
- how the organizer will handle an unavailable or undeliverable prize.

The Replit submission form does not collect a shipping address or email. Do not request an address in a public comment or store it in the public card archive. Collect only the minimum delivery information through an owner-approved private process after the winner accepts.

## 6. Separation from paid products

The optional $2 four-second Replit template motion card is a separate creator product available only after submission approval. Optional motion cards and merchandise do not affect entry, votes, rankings, eligibility, the Base44 result, or selection of the physical-prize winner.

## 7. Required approvals before promotion

The organizer must not announce or promote this physical prize until all of the following are complete:

1. Base44's official rules are updated to include the cross-era final and approved prize terms.
2. The owner verifies the winner in Base44 and approves that winner's print-ready card artwork.
3. A printer capable of the approved single-card format is selected and a private proof is approved.
4. Qualified counsel reviews the final official rules, eligibility, voting and tie procedures, notification, shipping, and required disclosures.

**Status: paused and inactive by organizer decision.** This draft does not activate a prize, promise delivery, amend Base44's published rules, or authorize a mockup, order, or promotion.
